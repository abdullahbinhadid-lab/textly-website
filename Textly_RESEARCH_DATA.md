## User Pain Points (Text Tools Niche)

- wordcounter.net — "more ads than actual content. Banner ads, sidebar ads, pop-ups fighting for your attention"
- Scroll to see stats — "Almost every word counter uses text input on top, statistics below. You have to scroll down to check, scroll back up to edit"
- Privacy silence — "Most word counter sites don't even address the question. Check their privacy policies — you won't find a single line saying 'your text is processed locally'"
- Numbers without context — "Showing '1,247 words' tells you nothing useful. What I actually wanted to know was: does this fit in a tweet? How far am I from my professor's 1,500-word requirement?"
- Word count bugs — punctuation counted as words, contractions counted as multiple words, smart apostrophes vs plain apostrophes give different counts
